What changed when Markou Design London was rebuilt?

A transparent before-and-after record of six technical and search-readiness metrics from the Markou Design London website rebuild. The results show meaningful progress, while the methodology notes explain exactly what this dataset can—and cannot—prove.

01 / Result

What moved?

The rebuild improved every recorded measure. Mobile performance increased from 63 to 90, while First Contentful Paint fell from 4.3 seconds to 0.9 seconds.

The largest remaining performance constraint was LCP. It improved by 5.9 seconds, from 9.1 to 3.2 seconds; however, 3.2 seconds was still above Google's published 2.5-second “good” threshold and should not be presented as finished work.

02 / Method

How should the numbers be read?

This is a diagnostic case-study dataset, not a controlled industry benchmark. It records the supplied summary values from before and after one client rebuild.

The public source material did not retain raw Lighthouse exports, audit timestamps, device profiles or tool-version identifiers. Therefore, the dataset reports the values accurately but does not claim laboratory reproducibility or field-performance equivalence.

What is FCP?

First Contentful Paint measures the time from navigation until the first text, image, SVG or non-white canvas content is rendered. The official web.dev guidance describes 1.8 seconds or less as a good experience at the 75th percentile.

What is LCP?

Largest Contentful Paint reports when the largest visible image, text block or video is rendered. The official web.dev guidance sets 2.5 seconds or less as the good threshold at the 75th percentile.

What are AEO and GEO scores?

These are third-party diagnostic scores for answer-engine and generative-engine readiness. Unlike Core Web Vitals, they are not standardised web-platform metrics, so they should be interpreted within the scoring tool rather than compared across unrelated tools.

Limitation: lab and diagnostic scores can change with network conditions, device emulation, test location, page state and tool versions. Re-test the live site independently before relying on these values for procurement or research.

About

Founder of Rock & Blu Ltd | Lead Technologist

I’m Satyam Patil, Founder and Lead Technologist of Rock & Blu Ltd, a UK software development and IT consultancy. I began building the studio in December 2025 and incorporated it in England and Wales on 6 February 2026.

Since starting Rock & Blu, I have led seven client engagements across bespoke interiors, creative services, hospitality, AI publishing, fashion ecommerce, education technology and experiential WebGL. I lead strategy, UX, software architecture, development, automation, performance, accessibility and Google and AI-search optimisation.

Before Rock & Blu, I worked at Wipro as a Project Engineer and Senior Project Engineer, delivering Anaplan planning models, data integrations, KPI dashboards and user training. That combination of enterprise systems experience and creative technology now shapes how I build distinctive, scalable digital products.
